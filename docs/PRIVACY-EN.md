# Better visual Youtube — Privacy policy

Developer: QuanNguyen. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Better visual Youtube adds glass effects, animations and Ambient Light to YouTube on desktop. You can use it without creating an account.

## Information processed on your device

The extension reads the YouTube page to apply its styles. When colour-based effects are enabled, it processes video and thumbnail colours on your device. Your settings and operational preferences are saved locally in your browser profile.

The extension does not send browsing history, account credentials, video frames or settings to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising analytics or remote crash-report uploads. Diagnostic errors may be written to the local browser console.

## Network activity and third parties

**Optional live sky:** only after you grant the optional Open-Meteo access, enabling Live sun & weather sends the coordinates of your selected city to api.open-meteo.com. Explicit city searches send the entered city name to geocoding-api.open-meteo.com. These services also receive your IP address as part of a normal connection. Requests omit credentials and referrers; they do not contain YouTube page URLs, video information or account data. The extension does not use GPS or automatically determine your location. The chosen city and coordinates are stored locally. One recent weather response and an attempt timestamp are cached locally, shared across tabs; weather normally refreshes every 15 minutes while a visible Home, Search, or Channel page uses it. If fetching fails, the scene may use cached weather up to two hours old. See [Open-Meteo terms and privacy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms). Weather is model-based, not direct live observation. Turning live weather off stops new automatic requests; it does not revoke the browser permission or erase the local cache.

YouTube continues to make its normal network requests under its own policies. Ambient Light may also request storyboard images from YouTube to process video colours. External links, such as support and documentation, are covered by those websites' policies.

When you uninstall the extension, your browser opens an optional feedback page hosted on GitHub Pages. Its fixed URL contains no user identifier, browsing history or settings. GitHub processes normal website request information under its own privacy policy. The page has no analytics and does not automatically submit or save your answers.

You may copy your feedback or open a draft in your email app. Feedback reaches QuanNguyen only if you send the email yourself; this shares the message and the sender information supplied by your email provider. Sent feedback is kept in the support mailbox. Contact the address below to request its removal.

## Permissions

- Storage: retain settings locally between sessions.
- Access to www.youtube.com: apply styles, discover the player and produce the chosen visual effects.
- Optional access to api.open-meteo.com and geocoding-api.open-meteo.com: fetch chosen-city weather and city-search results, only when you enable/use those features. You can revoke it in the browser's extension site-access controls.

The extension does not request access to all websites or permission to read browser history.

## Control and retention

You can disable individual effects or the extension. Reset to Defaults replaces the local configuration with the bundled defaults; it does not erase all operational storage. Removing the extension through your browser removes its local extension storage according to the browser's behavior. This extension does not provide automatic cross-device settings synchronization.
