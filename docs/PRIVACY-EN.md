# Better visual Youtube — Privacy policy

Developer: QuanNguyen. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Better visual Youtube adds glass effects, animations and Ambient Light to YouTube on desktop. You can use it without creating an account.

## Information processed on your device

The extension reads the YouTube page to apply its styles. When colour-based effects are enabled, it processes video and thumbnail colours on your device. Your settings and operational preferences are saved locally in your browser profile.

The extension does not send browsing history, account credentials, video frames or settings to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising analytics or remote crash-report uploads. Diagnostic errors may be written to the local browser console.

## Network activity and third parties

YouTube continues to make its normal network requests under its own policies. Ambient Light may also request storyboard images from YouTube to process video colours. External links, such as support and documentation, open when you select them and are covered by those websites' policies.

## Permissions

- Storage: retain settings locally between sessions.
- Access to www.youtube.com: apply styles, discover the player and produce the chosen visual effects.

The extension does not request access to all websites or permission to read browser history.

## Control and retention

You can disable individual effects or the extension. Reset to Defaults replaces the local configuration with the bundled defaults; it does not erase all operational storage. Removing the extension through your browser removes its local extension storage according to the browser's behavior. This extension does not provide automatic cross-device settings synchronization.
